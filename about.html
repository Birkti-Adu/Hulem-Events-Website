Ashenda Addis Ababa 2026

Where Tradition Meets Experience

Ashenda Experience 2026 was a celebration of Ethiopian culture, bringing together tradition, music, dance, fashion, and community in Addis Ababa. Created and organized by Hulem Events and hosted at Yehulu Bar & Restaurant, the event aimed to create a space where people could celebrate their heritage, reconnect with their culture, and share the joy of Ashenda together.

Rooted in the spirit of this cherished cultural celebration, the experience brought people together through traditional dance, music, cultural expression, and fashion. Every element contributed to an atmosphere filled with energy, pride, and connection, honoring the traditions of Ashenda while presenting them through a contemporary event experience.

Hulem Events led the event’s concept development, planning, creative direction, coordination, promotion, and on-site execution. The experience was further supported through collaborations with Hulem Kemis and Yaya Pictures, bringing together fashion, creativity, and visual storytelling to help capture the spirit of the celebration.

Ashenda Experience 2026 marked an important milestone in Hulem Events’ journey. It reflected our belief that events can do more than entertain: they can preserve cultural identity, strengthen community connections, and create memories that stay with people long after the celebration ends.

For Hulem Events, Ashenda was not simply an event to organize. It was an opportunity to celebrate culture, bring people together, and turn a meaningful tradition into a shared experience.
