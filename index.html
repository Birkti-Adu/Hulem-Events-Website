Hulem Events Featured on Ye Noha Hasab Podcast

Sharing the Story Behind Hulem

Being invited to Ye Noha Hasab Podcast was a meaningful moment for Hulem Events. It gave us the opportunity to sit down, talk openly about how Hulem started, the ideas behind our work, and the experiences we are building through events.

During the conversation, we shared our journey across cultural events, creative experiences, entertainment, and community focused projects, as well as our vision for where Hulem Events is going.

The conversation reached an even wider audience through social media, with selected clips from the podcast receiving 150,000+ views on TikTok. For us, it was more than a number. It was an opportunity for more people to discover Hulem, understand what we do, and connect with the vision behind the brand.

The feature became a special milestone in Hulem’s journey, giving us a platform to tell our story, celebrate the work we have already created, and introduce the next chapter of Hulem Events to a growing audience.
